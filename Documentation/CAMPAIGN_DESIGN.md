so that Chapter 1 sets them up correctly. **Do not build them before Chapter 1
is validated.**

## Chapter 1 — City Without Tomorrow (beat sheet)

Target length: 35–50 minutes for a first-time 4-player group.
Vertical slice = beats 0–7 in one district.

| Beat | Name | Play | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **The Last Signal** (cinematic) | Non-interactive, ~2:30, skippable after first view | Tone, stakes, the fracture |
| 1 | **Waking** | Each player wakes on the Arrival Steps in their own reality. Short movement tutorial within sight of the clock tower | Learn movement; notice "my world looks different" |
| 2 | **Where are you?** | Players are close but cannot see each other's worlds; spatial voice/pings still work. Objective: "Find the others" → stand together at the clock | Discover the core mechanic through play |
| 3 | **Four Clocks** | Each describes the clock tower; HUD confirms the realities by name | Name the realities; set the communication habit |
| 4 | **Sealed Hall** | Echo follows a filament to a hidden switch → Present's hall door unseals. Present fetches the power cell; Past reads the lectern | First cross-reality cause and effect; one interaction per reality |
| 5 | **The Causeway** | Future guides everyone across the collapsing causeway | Future's role; trust |
| 6 | **The Meridian Gate** | The co-op puzzle (GAMEPLAY_DESIGN §6). First lockout or halfway point: **MORROW speaks** for the first time through the gate's PA | Core co-op test; MORROW introduction |
| 7 | **The Gate Opens** (in-engine cinematic) | A transmission; MORROW: *"You were never supposed to remember."*; the gate opens; a figure in the distance; cut to black; "End of Chapter 1" | Hook for Chapter 2 |

### MORROW first-contact script (draft, beat 6)
> MORROW: "Please stop."
> MORROW: "You are pressing on a wound you cannot see. Each of you holds one quarter of something that was meant to stay in pieces."
> *(if they continue)* MORROW: "I have closed this gate four hundred and eleven times. I would prefer not to make it four hundred and twelve."
> *(on lockout)* MORROW: "Rest. I will be here when you try again. I am always here."

### Chapter 1 ending (draft, beat 7)
> *Gate panel flares. A broken transmission: Elara's voice, reversed, then clear for one word: "...remember..."*
> MORROW: "You were never supposed to remember."
> *The gate opens onto the Engine district. Far down the causeway, a figure
> stands still in all four realities at once, the first thing every player
> sees identically. It raises one hand. Cut to black.*
> **END OF CHAPTER ONE — CITY WITHOUT TOMORROW**

Identity of the figure: PROPOSED to be Elara Voss (or an echo of her);
alternatively Nyra. Owner decision required (DECISIONS.md, D-07).

### Expansion after slice acceptance (Phase 6, not before)
Additional Chapter 1 spaces: tram tunnels (Present/Future route puzzle), the
founders' lamp-harbour (Past-led sequence), MORROW's relay station (first
"Convergence Point" with temporary phasing grants), and Kael's maintenance channel.
