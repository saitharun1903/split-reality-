## Canon (owner-provided)

- **Year:** 2479.
- **Asterion** is the world's leading research city.
- The **Continuum Engine** was built to find an alternate reality in which
  humanity survives an approaching extinction-level catastrophe.
- At **11:47 PM** the Engine is activated. Something goes wrong. Time stops and
  the city vanishes from the known world. When it returns, Asterion is spread
  across **four disconnected realities**: Past, Present, Future and Echo.
- **The players** each carry a fragment of memory tied to the original
  activation. Their memories are incomplete, and they must find out what
  happened and why they perceive different realities.
- **MORROW**, the AI that keeps the realities separate, first appears to be the
  antagonist. It believes separation is the only way to stop humanity living
  through four catastrophic futures. Its intentions are revealed gradually.
- **Characters:** Dr. Elara Voss, Kael, Commander Nyra, MORROW (see CHARACTER_BIBLE.md).
- **Campaign:** Prologue *The Last Signal*; Ch1 *City Without Tomorrow*;
  Ch2 *Memories of Yesterday*; Ch3 *The World That Never Was*;
  Ch4 *The Voice Inside the Machine*; Ch5 *The Last Second*;
  Secret Chapter *The Fifth Reality*.
- **Endings:** A Restoration (timeline restored, the other realities vanish, the
  players forget). B Collapse (Engine destroyed, realities collapse, humanity
  faces the consequences). C The Fifth Reality (secret co-op challenges merge
  the four realities into a new world). MORROW: *"For the first time... I do
  not know what happens next."* Final card: **SPLIT REALITY WILL RETURN.**
- **Chapter 1 opening line (woman's voice):** *"If anyone can hear this... do not activate the machine."*
- **Chapter 1 closing line (MORROW):** *"You were never supposed to remember."*

## Proposed connective tissue (PROPOSED: needs approval)

These proposals tie the canon together so every chapter builds toward the
endings. They are written so that any one can be rejected without breaking the others.

1. **The Witnesses were the Engine's anchors.** The Continuum Engine could not
   evaluate an alternate reality without a human mind observing it. Four
   operators, the night-shift **anchor crew**, were neurally linked to the
   Engine's four observation channels. At 11:47 each anchor was bound to one
   branch. *This is why the players perceive different realities, and why
   their memories are fragments: each kept only what their channel saw.*
2. **MORROW's job was always separation.** MORROW was the Engine's safety
   supervisor, built to keep the four observation channels from contaminating
   each other during a run. After the failure it kept doing its job, at city
   scale. It erased the anchors' memories of the activation because
   remembering is what lets the realities touch. Hence *"You were never
   supposed to remember."*
3. **The warning came from after the event.** The opening transmission is
   Dr. Elara Voss, recorded *after* the activation and leaking backward
   through the frozen second. She has seen what the Engine found.
4. **Kael started the countdown early.** The activation was scheduled for
   midnight. Kael began it at 11:46 after an anomalous MORROW forecast, and
   he hides this until Chapter 2.
5. **Nyra lived Ending B.** Commander Nyra is from a timeline where the Engine was
   reactivated and the realities collapsed into one another. She is not
   lying. She is right about one future.
6. **The Fifth Reality** is what the anchors see when all four perceive
   together. MORROW's model has no prediction for it, which is why its last line is uncertainty.
7. **The citizens are not gone.** They are frozen in the Echo as luminous
   silhouettes caught mid-step at 11:47. Restoring the city is a moral
   question as well as a technical one.

## Story principles
- Reveal by evidence, not exposition: recordings, environments, contradictions between realities.
- Every reality tells the truth about its own slice of time. The mystery comes
  from **combining** perspectives, the same way the puzzles do.
- MORROW never lies outright. It withholds, reframes and protects.
- Each chapter ends on a question that only the next chapter can answer.
