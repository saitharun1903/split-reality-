# SPLIT REALITY — Game Vision

**Subtitle:** The Silence Between Seconds
**Tagline:** Four Players. Four Realities. One World.
**Genre:** Cinematic sci-fi co-op adventure: mystery, puzzle, exploration and narrative action
**Engine:** Unreal Engine 5 · **Platforms:** PC (lead), Android, Web (separate workstreams)

## The one-sentence pitch
Four players stand in the same frozen square of a vanished city, and each one
sees a different version of it. No one can finish alone, and no one can see
what the others describe.

## Pillars
1. **Asymmetric perception is the game.** Every major beat depends on
   information only some players can see. If a puzzle can be solved by one
   player looking around, it is cut.
2. **Talk, don't point.** Players cannot see each other's clues, so they have to
   describe, translate and trust. The game is designed around voice and pings
   (see GAMEPLAY_DESIGN.md).
3. **A city with a history.** Asterion has to feel lived in, in four eras of
   one place. Every room answers "what happened here?"
4. **Cinematic but interactive.** Cut-scenes frame the play and never replace it.
   We prefer in-engine moments players can move through.
5. **Small and excellent.** One district done exceptionally well beats a
   hollow open world. Chapter 1 gets polished before Chapter 2 exists.

## What success looks like for Chapter 1
- Players finish the Meridian Gate and say "wait, so what did *you* see?"
- Players can tell which reality a screenshot comes from without a HUD.
- Two players can finish it, and four players find it richer.
- The last line ("You were never supposed to remember") makes people want Chapter 2.

## Explicit non-goals (for now)
- No combat system in the vertical slice. Danger comes from the environment
  (Future hazards, surges), not enemies.
- No monetisation of any kind before the core game is proven.
  Later monetisation is cosmetic only (see MONETISATION section in ROADMAP.md).
- No open world. Chapter 1 is a hand-built district.
- No single build that targets all three platforms.

## Originality guardrails
Asterion, MORROW, the Continuum Engine, the Witnesses and the reality
mechanics are original. We do not reproduce characters, maps, art, dialogue or
signature mechanics from existing games. Reference boards may cite real
architecture, photography and film language, never another game's assets.
