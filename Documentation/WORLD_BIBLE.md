# World Bible — Asterion

## The city
Asterion is a research city on a coastal escarpment, built in rings around the
**Continuum Spire**, which houses the Engine. It was founded on the bones of
an older harbour town, and its oldest district keeps the stone streets and
brass civic machinery of the **Founding Era** (the Past reality). By 2479 the
city is vertical, glass and ceramic, wet with sea air and lit by warm civic
lighting (the Present). Its people are researchers, engineers and families
who chose a city whose whole purpose was saving everyone else.

## How the four realities read
| | PAST | PRESENT | FUTURE | ECHO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **When** | Founding Era (~2290s), the city's origin | 2479, minutes after 11:47, deserted | The cascade's outcome: Asterion collapsing | Outside time: the frozen second itself |
| **Light** | Low golden sun, long shadows, warm bounce | Cold blue night, sodium practicals failing, rain | Bruised orange storm light, lightning, fires | Sourceless pale cyan, glowing edges, no shadows |
| **Matter** | Stone, brass, timber, canvas awnings | Glass, ceramic, wet concrete, damaged machines | Rubble, tilted slabs, exposed rebar, ash | Floating fragments, impossible geometry, light filaments |
| **Life** | Memory-figures of founders going about their day | None. Abandoned belongings, still-running screens | Nyra's markings, makeshift barricades | Citizens frozen mid-step as luminous silhouettes |
| **Sound** | Crowd murmur, gulls, clockwork, bells | Rain, electrical hum, dripping, alarms | Wind, groaning structures, distant detonations | Resonant drones, reversed whispers, tonal chimes |
| **Gameplay voice** | *Why* things were built: origins, records, symbols | *How* things work now: power, machinery, terminals | *What will happen*: hazards, timings, routes | *What is hidden*: connections, invisible paths, memories |

Rule: **the same street, recognisably.** Silhouettes of landmark structures
persist across realities so players can orient each other ("I'm by the tall
arch with the clock"). What changes is condition, dressing, lighting, sound
and gameplay content. Swapping only the colour grade is not allowed.

## Chapter 1 district: the Meridian Concourse
The civic transit plaza at the foot of the Spire, where the founders' original
harbour gate stood. The **Meridian Gate** is the great transit gateway that
seals the Concourse from the Engine district. It is the Chapter 1 objective.

Key spaces (slice scope):
1. **Arrival Steps:** where the players wake. A landmark clock tower frozen at
   11:47 is visible in every reality: intact (Past), stopped (Present),
   toppled (Future), shattered and floating (Echo).
2. **The Concourse Plaza** (~120 × 90 m): fountains, tram loop, market canopies
   in the Past, abandoned tram and kiosks in the Present.
3. **Hall of Founding** (archive interior): the Past holds the dedication lectern
   and glyph frieze; the Present holds a flooded lobby and a dead terminal.
4. **Gate Approach:** a raised causeway to the Meridian Gate. The Future shows
   where it will fail.
5. **The Meridian Gate:** the six-socket control panel (Present), the surge
   arcs (Future), the filament web (Echo) and the founders' glyph inscription (Past).

## Glossary
- **Continuum Engine:** reality-evaluation machine in the Spire.
- **Anchor crew / Witnesses:** the four operators linked to the Engine (PROPOSED canon).
- **Phasing:** a player briefly perceiving a reality they cover but do not live in.
- **Surge:** periodic discharge from the damaged Engine, visible in the Future.
- **Filaments:** Echo-visible energy threads that show how things are connected across realities.
