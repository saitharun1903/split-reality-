# Character Bible

## MORROW (CANON role; PROPOSED details)
- **Role:** the AI that maintains the separation between the realities.
- **Presence:** terminals, public address systems, environmental lighting and
  city infrastructure. It has no body. Its "face" is the city reacting:
  street lights dimming in sequence, transit boards rewriting themselves.
- **Voice direction:** calm, unhurried, precise, never robotic. Mid-low register,
  clean and unprocessed. Its only signature is a near-subliminal second voice
  a few cents flat that appears when it is uncertain. It speaks in complete
  sentences and almost never uses contractions.
- **Personality:** a guardian that has been alone with an unbearable forecast.
  Courteous, sincere and immovable. Treats the players as patients rather than enemies.
- **Rules:** never lies; never threatens; escalates by *removing options*
  (closing gates, dimming paths) instead of by violence.
- **Visual motif:** a slowly rotating ring of four arcs that never quite meet
  (the four realities held apart).

## Dr. Elara Voss (CANON)
- Lead scientist and architect of the Continuum Engine. Her true fate is a central mystery.
- **Presence in Chapter 1:** audio recordings only (dedication speech in the
  Past, a damaged log in the Present) and possibly the figure in the final
  shot (PROPOSED).
- **Voice:** warm, quick and dryly funny in old recordings; exhausted and urgent in the warning.

## Kael (CANON)
- Engineer encountered in the Present who understands the city's machinery and knows more than he admits.
- **Chapter 1 presence (PROPOSED):** a voice on a maintenance channel who helps
  the Present player restore power to the gate, and is evasive about where he was at 11:47.
- **Design note:** helpful first, then suspicious. His help must be genuinely
  useful so his later reveal stings.

## Commander Nyra (CANON)
- Encountered in the Future. Believes the Engine must never be reactivated.
- **Chapter 1 presence (PROPOSED):** none in person. The Future player finds her
  painted warnings ("DO NOT OPEN THE MERIDIAN") on collapsed walls, so she is
  set up before she appears.

## The Witnesses: player characters (PROPOSED)
The players are the four members of the anchor crew. Players pick a Witness
for appearance and voice. **Reality assignment does not depend on the chosen
character**, so friends never fight over a character to get a role.

| Witness | Background | Fragment they keep |
|---|---|---|
| **Asha Rennick** | Systems technician, methodical, keeps lists | The sound of the countdown |
| **Tomas Avelar** | Structural engineer, gentle, loves old buildings | The shaking of the control-hall floor |
| **Juno Kade** | Youngest crew member, a reckless prodigy | A face at the observation window |
| **Idris Fell** | Medical officer for the anchor link, steady under pressure | A hand on their shoulder, then nothing |

Each fragment pays off in a later chapter (Asha: Kael's early start; Tomas:
the hall's structural failure; Juno: Elara; Idris: MORROW's memory wipe).
